FAQ

Common pricing questions

Can I use EasyHisab only on my phone?

Yes on mobile for every plan. Basic and Pro also include the signed-in web portal for desktop workflows.

Do I need Pro for GST invoices?

No. GST billing is included on all plans on mobile. Pro adds more businesses and optional paid top-ups.

What happens when I hit my business limit?

You can keep using existing businesses. Creating a new business beyond your plan limit is blocked until you upgrade.

How do I upgrade or get a quote?

Email easyhisabsupport@gmail.com with your account email and the plan you are interested in.
