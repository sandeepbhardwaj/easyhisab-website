Access

Who can sign in to the web portal?

  • Basic and Pro subscribers — web portal is included with an active paid plan.
  • Invited Free users — time-limited access may be granted for marketing or beta; contact support.
  • Free without invite — mobile continues to work; web shows an upgrade path to Basic or Pro.

Sign in with the same Google account you use on Android. Your businesses, invoices, and settings sync through the billing service — there is no separate web-only account.

Trust

Same financial rules as mobile

Issued documents are immutable. Totals and GST splits come from the server. Invoice numbers are allocated at issue time and never reused.

Immutable issued docs

Corrections use cancellation or credit notes — never silent edits to issued invoices.

Server-computed totals

CGST, SGST, IGST, and line totals are calculated on the billing service, not the browser.

FY-scoped numbering

Invoice numbers are allocated at issue time and stay unique within each financial year.

Ready for Basic or Pro?

Basic unlocks the web portal with purchase orders on up to three businesses. Pro adds up to five businesses.
